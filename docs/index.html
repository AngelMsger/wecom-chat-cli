WHEN THE MODEL GETS IT WRONG

Limit what can happen next.

Safety must not depend on the worker recognizing every malicious instruction.

01 / SCOPE

A grant, not an inbox.

Bind a short-lived task to one event, sender, session epoch and connection. Revoke it or reset the session to invalidate stale work.

Session routing ↗
02 / REVIEW

A draft, not a send.

Free text needs independent approval of its immutable digest. Unattended replies require explicitly configured literal templates.

Reply policies ↗
03 / MEMORY

A report, not a ban.

A worker can decline a risky task. It cannot silence a person in future conversations or change their permissions.

Risk reports ↗

Host isolation still matters. Keep privileged tools, private data and other workers’ tokens outside the conversation worker. Read the security model ↗
